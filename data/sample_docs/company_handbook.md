# Brightleaf Labs Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

## 1. Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours,
employees may organise their schedule freely as long as they work 40 hours per week.

## 2. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require written approval from a department head. The company reimburses up to
USD 500 per year for home-office equipment.

## 3. Vacation and leave
- Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
- Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year; they expire on March 31.
- Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.
- Sick leave is unlimited, but a doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for all parents.

## 4. Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of USD 1,200 for courses, books and
conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## 5. Expenses
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase, with receipts attached.
Travel must be booked through the company travel portal. Economy class is the
default for flights under 6 hours.
